
# Chapter 2: Literature Survey

## 2.1 Existing Crowdfunding Platforms

A review of existing crowdfunding platforms was conducted to understand the current landscape, identify common features, and pinpoint areas for improvement.

*   **GoFundMe:** One of the largest global platforms, primarily for personal causes and emergencies. It has a simple-to-use interface but has faced scrutiny over campaign verification and fraud prevention. Its strength lies in its social sharing features.
*   **Kickstarter:** A popular platform focused on creative projects (films, games, art). It operates on an "all-or-nothing" funding model, where projects only receive funds if they meet their target. This reduces risk for backers but is not suitable for all types of campaigns, especially charitable ones.
*   **Ketto & Milaap:** These are prominent crowdfunding platforms in India. They have successfully integrated local payment methods like UPI. Their focus is largely on medical and social causes. While effective, their user interface and campaign creation tools follow a traditional web model.

The analysis reveals that while existing platforms are successful, there is a clear opportunity to innovate in the areas of user assistance (through AI), enhanced security, and more streamlined user experiences.

## 2.2 Research on AI in Crowdfunding

The application of Artificial Intelligence in crowdfunding is an emerging field. Research indicates several potential benefits:

*   **Predicting Campaign Success:** Studies by scholars like Kuppuswamy and Bayus have shown that machine learning models can predict the likelihood of a campaign's success by analyzing its text, funding goal, and category.
*   **Fraud Detection:** AI algorithms can be trained to identify patterns associated with fraudulent campaigns, such as unusual wording, suspicious account activity, or unrealistic goals. This can significantly enhance platform security.
*   **Personalized Recommendations:** AI can be used to recommend campaigns to potential donors based on their past giving history and interests, increasing engagement and donation frequency.
*   **Natural Language Generation (NLG):** Recent advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs) make it possible to automatically generate high-quality text. This can be harnessed to help users write better campaign descriptions, as demonstrated in this project.

## 2.3 The Research Gap

The literature review identified a significant gap: while AI's potential in crowdfunding is recognized academically, its practical application on existing platforms, especially for aiding campaigners, is still limited. Most platforms leave the crucial task of content creation entirely to the user. There is a lack of tools that actively assist a user in crafting a compelling and emotionally resonant campaign story.

This project aims to fill this gap by integrating a generative AI tool directly into the campaign creation workflow.

## 2.4 Problem Statement

Traditional crowdfunding platforms require users to manually create all campaign content, which can be a barrier for those lacking strong writing or marketing skills. This often leads to lower-quality campaigns that fail to attract donors. Furthermore, concerns about fraudulent activities can deter potential contributors.

The problem is to develop a crowdfunding platform that **empowers users by providing AI-driven assistance for content creation** and is **designed with a foundation for enhanced security**, thereby improving campaign effectiveness and building donor trust.
